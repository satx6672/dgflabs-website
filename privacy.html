DGF Middleware Edge Proxy — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

DGF Labs ("we", "our") develops and operates the DGF Middleware Edge Proxy browser extension (v1.4.8+). The extension is architected as a local-first browser-edge membrane with strict data minimization and no user account requirement. This policy describes exactly what data stays on your device and what minimal non-personal state is exchanged with our servers.

2. Local-Only Data (Never Leaves Your Browser)

All active boundary enforcement runs locally inside Chrome via Manifest V3 declarativeNetRequest rulesets and a packaged local script (defuser.js).

  • Live Interception Ledger: The rolling 30-entry audit log displayed in your local Edge Governance Console and toolbar popup (timestamps, resource vector types, blocked third-party origins, and initiating hostnames) is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) and is never transmitted to DGF Labs or any third party. You can clear this ledger at any time.
  • Enterprise Managed Policies: If deployed in a managed corporate environment, read-only custom domain blocklists may be supplied locally by your organization's administrator via Chrome's chrome.storage.managed API.

3. Minimal Server Communication (Zero Browsing History)

The extension communicates with the DGF backend (digital-governance-framework.onrender.com) solely for three narrowly scoped technical functions:

  • Anonymous License Verification: On install, the extension generates a random local UUID (installation_uuid) stored in chrome.storage.local. This anonymous identifier is checked against /license/{uuid} to verify lifetime license activation and is passed as client_reference_id when you choose to open Stripe Checkout. No email address, name, or account is required by the extension.
  • Ephemeral Session Continuity: For activated installations, the extension requests a rotating 4-hour anonymous continuity token (continuity_ref) via /continuity.
  • Rate-Limited Aggregate Signal (SRV): For activated installations, at most once every 60 seconds during active boundary drops, the extension emits a non-identifying aggregate signal to /srv containing the ephemeral continuity token, harm classification (Class1_DataExfiltration), timestamp, and cumulative drop count. No visited URLs, blocked domain names, page contents, search queries, or personally identifiable information (PII) are ever included in these transmissions.

4. Payment Processing

Lifetime license purchases are processed externally by Stripe. DGF Labs does not collect, process, or store your payment card details inside the browser extension. Stripe's handling of payment data is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Data Sale

We do not sell, rent, or transfer user data to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics platforms. We do not collect browsing history or build behavioral profiles.

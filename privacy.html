DGF Middleware Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026

1. Overview

DGF Labs ("we", "our") operates the DGF Middleware Edge Proxy browser extension. We are committed to local-first security and strict data minimization. This policy explains how our extension handles data.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The DGF Middleware operates primarily as a local-first transport layer. To establish cryptographic session continuity, the extension communicates with our proprietary backend servers.

  • Telemetry and State Data: The extension generates and routes temporary cryptographic tokens (such as continuity references and action states) to our backend to execute stateful sequential validation.
  • No Behavioral Harvesting: We do not pool, monetize, or track your personal browsing history, search queries, or identity.

3. Data Storage

Cryptographic session tokens are stored locally on your device using standard Manifest V3 local storage APIs. Server-side provisional states are ephemeral and exist only to validate the immediate cryptographic handshake.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

5. Contact

For questions regarding this policy or the Digital Governance Framework architecture, please contact us at dgflabs@github.com.
